# B2B SaaS GTM Pipeline & ICP Analytics — Findings

**Synthetic data only.** Seed 84; 8,000 CRM leads.

## KPI snapshot
- Closed-won rate: 4.3%
- Won deals: 343
- Closed-won contract value: $4,462,701
- Average ACV: $13,011
- Average sales cycle: 43.5 days

## Channel finding
**Founder Outbound** has the strongest simulated win rate at 7.8%, with average ACV $12,906 and 40.1-day cycle.

## ICP finding
A high-quality ICP hypothesis is **201+ Services accounts**, with 7.9% win rate, $30,882 average ACV and 47.3-day average sales cycle.

## Recommendations to test
1. Narrow outbound messaging around the highest-quality ICP pain point and compare demo-to-win conversion.
2. Use channel-quality scoring that combines win rate, ACV and cycle time instead of raw lead count.
3. Review lost reasons by persona and stage to identify message, urgency and budget objections.

## Limitations
All results are simulated and the channel/ICP differences are intentionally embedded for learning. The analysis demonstrates method, not real sales performance.
