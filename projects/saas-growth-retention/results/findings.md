# SaaS Growth & Retention Intelligence — Findings

**Synthetic data only.** Seed 42; 3,500 B2B SaaS accounts.

## KPI snapshot
- Activation rate: 65.7%
- Activated-to-paid conversion: 51.6%
- Paid accounts: 1,188
- Paid churn rate in the observation window: 30.1%
- Simulated monthly recurring revenue: $146,352
- Average revenue per paid account: $123

## Segmentation findings
- Highest paid churn channel: **Paid Search (43.5%)**
- Lowest paid churn channel: **Partner (21.2%)**
- A low-churn/high-value segment in this simulation: **51-200 accounts via Referral**, churn 12.2%, average MRR $117.

## Recommendations to test
1. Compare onboarding completion, time-to-value and acquisition intent for paid-search accounts before reducing budget.
2. Build a role/industry-specific onboarding experiment for the low-churn ICP segment.
3. Use feature-adoption thresholds as an early-warning signal, then validate predictive power on a holdout set.

## Limitations
All data is simulated. Segment differences are intentionally embedded so the project can demonstrate analytical workflow. The findings are not evidence about a real company and should not be presented as causal.
