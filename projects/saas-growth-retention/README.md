# SaaS Growth & Retention Intelligence

A recruiter-ready portfolio project for **Data Analyst, Product Analyst and Growth/GTM** roles.

## Business question
Which customer segments activate fastest, retain longest, and are most likely to expand or churn?

## Stack
Python, SQL, Power BI-ready outputs, cohort analysis, product metrics, ICP segmentation.

## Dataset
Synthetic B2B SaaS account data with 3,500 accounts. No real customer data.

## What this project demonstrates
- Activation and trial-to-paid conversion
- Cohort retention
- Churn analysis by plan, company size, industry and acquisition channel
- Feature-adoption analysis
- MRR / ARPU
- ICP hypothesis generation
- Decision-oriented recommendations with explicit limitations

## Key results
- Activation: **65.7%**
- Activated → paid: **51.6%**
- Paid churn: **30.1%**
- Simulated MRR: **$146,352**

Open `dashboard/index.html` to view the static interactive-style dashboard.

## Files
- `data/accounts.csv` — synthetic account-level dataset
- `sql/` — activation, retention and churn queries
- `results/` — cohort/segment outputs and findings
- `dashboard/index.html` — portfolio dashboard

## Important
All results are from simulated data and are included to demonstrate method, not real business performance.
