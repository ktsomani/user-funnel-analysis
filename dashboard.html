Method, data quality & interpretation

Each stage requires an event strictly later than the previous stage in the same session. Repeated events count once. Missing steps, equal timestamps, and out-of-order purchases cannot complete the funnel. Conversion is purchases divided by visits, not unique-user conversion.

Data is generated with seed 42. Mobile checkout friction and channel differences are deliberately simulated. These results demonstrate a workflow and do not describe a real store. Wilson 95% intervals assume independent sessions; repeat users can violate that assumption. Differences are descriptive, not causal.

Next investigations: product-page friction, mobile payment errors, and traffic intent. Validate with real instrumentation and randomized experiments before claiming an improvement.
